# Contributor payments

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before requesting assignment. Record the role, scope and reward in the issue. Unassigned or unapproved work has no reward eligibility. Extra time does not increase a reward; agree scope and tier changes before doing more work.

## Rates and roles

All amounts are G$.

| Tier      |     Total |
| --------- | --------: |
| Basic     |   250,000 |
| Common    |   500,000 |
| Rare      | 2,000,000 |
| Epic      | 3,350,000 |
| Mythic    | 4,700,000 |
| Legendary | 6,000,000 |

- **Basic/Common:** contributor 100%; team review; any QA is separately priced.
- **Rare+:** lead 25%, contributor 37.5%, reviewer 30%, QA 7.5%. Combined review/QA receives 37.5% only when posted together. Otherwise these are different people; author self-tests never replace independent checks.
- Smart contracts or moving funds: minimum Rare. Maintainers appoint leads. An upgraded bounty keeps its contributor, uses the new split and requires appropriately eligible roles.
- QA Open: first-come assignment; after 48 hours unclaimed, a proven tester is assigned. QA Internal: team testing; reviewer retains 30%.
- Accepted bugs: 50,000 each, maximum five per bounty; reproducible, in scope and introduced by the PR.

## Request payment

After PR merge, or the agreed acceptance for standalone work:

1. Use a verified G$ wallet and [Gardens OS Contributors Pool](https://app.gardens.fund/gardens/42220/0x62b8b11039fcfe5ab0c56e502b1c372a3d2a9c7a/0xf42c9ca2b10010142e2bac34ebdddb0b82177684/94). Community membership requires a one-time 105,000 G$ stake.
2. Title the request with the bounty name. Include PR/report, issue, tier, role and payout address.
3. Await GoodDollar team approval.

The operator is the recipient/requester, including agent-assisted work. Never infer an unspecified standalone reward or approve your own payment.
